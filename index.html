vit2snn: layer-wise distillation of a Vision Transformer into a spiking ViT

Converts a pretrained ViT-Tiny/16 into a spiking Vision Transformer (4 time steps) in two phases: the student first copies the teacher layer by layer, then is fine-tuned on the labels with a distillation loss. Trained and evaluated on Imagenette on a laptop (Apple M2), with firing-rate profiling, an analytical energy estimate, diagnostics and a three-seed ablation study.

Layer-wise distillation adds +13.0 and +10.3 top-1 points over fine-tuning alone (5 and 12 epochs, positive in all 3 seeds). Best student: 70.2% top-1 on the 10 Imagenette classes with ~7.5% of neurons firing, at an estimated 0.56 mJ per image vs 5.77 mJ for the teacher.

Database-Sentinel: a multispectral dataset for satellite image compression

Independent research project with Prof. Diego Valsesia (Politecnico di Torino), building a large-scale multispectral Sentinel-2 dataset to train autoencoder models for onboard image compression. The pipeline builds a Sentinel-2 L1C time series for any point on Earth. It queries the Copernicus STAC API, filters images (clouds, coverage, quality of all 13 bands) and resamples them onto a 10 m UTM grid, logging a reason for every rejected image.

Comes with tests, a dataset datasheet and a site-selection bias analysis (WorldStrat).

Road scene segmentation and anomaly detection

Compared a pixel-based CNN (ERFNet) with a mask-based Transformer (EoMT, built on a self-supervised DINOv2 backbone) for semantic segmentation of road scenes and pixel-level detection of out-of-distribution objects. Fine-tuned EoMT with LoRA in its attention layers, and evaluated four post-hoc anomaly scores (MSP, Max Logit, Max Entropy, RbA) on 5 road benchmarks, with temperature-scaling calibration.

LoRA fine-tuning on Cityscapes raises the COCO-trained EoMT from 54.0% to 74.3% mIoU.

CentraleSupélec · Integration projectSep–Nov 2025 · team of 5

Cross-crypto trading signals from limit order books

Can Bitcoin's order book predict Ethereum's next moves? From millisecond bid/ask data, we aligned the asynchronous BTC and ETH order books, built 56 features (mid-prices, spreads, order-book imbalance, short-term volatility), measured lead-lag effects with transfer entropy, then backtested two strategies: a BTC → ETH lead-lag rule and a Random Forest classifier.

The BTC → ETH signal is statistically significant but not exploitable after costs: the Random Forest strategy goes from +4.8% without costs to −88.6% with 0.2% fees and 1 s latency.

TIPE · Lycée Louis-le-Grand2023–24

Navigating water currents with Markov decision processes

Modelled navigation through obstacles and water currents (e.g. a kayak in rapids) as a Markov decision process with stochastic transitions. Solved it exactly with value iteration (Bellman operator), then compared Q-learning and SARSA under ε-greedy exploration and tuned their learning and exploration rates.

Q-learning learns the shortest, riskier path; SARSA learns a safer detour away from obstacles.
